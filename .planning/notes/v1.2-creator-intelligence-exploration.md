# v1.2 Creator Intelligence — Exploration Notes

## Core Direction Shift

The product is moving from prescriptive ("here's what to buy this month based on category allocations") to informed ("here's what creators are backing right now, contextualised with market news — you decide"). The buy list mechanic survives but restructures around creators rather than asset categories.

## Key Decisions

### Creator Signals — Richer Extraction
Creator profiles must surface:
- Favoured stocks (specific tickers they back)
- Investment methodology (how they find stocks)
- Industry/sector focus (which sectors they're currently backing)
- Preferred index funds

### Two-Layer AI Extraction
Rather than a single transcript summary, AI produces two distinct layers:
- **Stable summary** — full 4-month window: established methodology, recurring themes, consistent picks
- **Latest signals** — last 30 days only: what the creator is focusing on right now, new picks, stance shifts

Both layers feed the watch list. Latest signals get higher weight in buy recommendations.

### Transcript Scraping Window
Only last 4 months of transcripts are scraped. Older content is not relevant to current creator stance.

### Watch List Replaces Buy List
The static buy list becomes a live watch list with real-time prices. Watch list items are drawn from creator picks (both layers). News flags appear per item.

### Per-Creator Budget Allocation
User sets £X/month per creator (not a % split across asset categories). The app translates this into actual share quantities to buy based on what that creator is currently backing at today's prices.
- Example: £200/month → Creator A's current picks → 3 shares ASML, 5 shares TSMC

### ISA Tab
Strip it completely. Not useful.

### Creator Enhancement Signals
- **Consensus** — when 2+ creators back the same ticker, surface as stronger conviction
- **Sentiment trend** — is the creator becoming more bullish or cautious over 4 months?
- **Contradiction detection** — flag when recent videos contradict older stance
- **Cadence weighting** — creators who haven't posted recently carry less weight automatically

### Creator Search
Users can search YouTube by creator name — no URL required. Carries over from Phase 9 (v1.1).

## Market News Integration

### Sources (layered)
- **Finnhub or Alpha Vantage news API** — ticker-specific news, free tier, finance-focused
- **RSS from 2-3 trusted outlets** — BBC Business, Reuters UK, FT headlines — UK macro context
- **Bank of England / ONS feeds** — interest rate decisions, inflation data

### What AI Extracts
- Ticker-level: earnings, analyst upgrades/downgrades, major announcements for watch list items
- Sector-level: headwinds/tailwinds for industries creators are currently backing
- Macro: UK interest rate direction, inflation trend, market sentiment

### How It Surfaces
- Each watch list item gets a news flag ("3 relevant stories this week")
- Creator card shows macro conditions for sectors that creator is backing
- Top-level **"this month's context"** summary: 3-4 Claude-generated sentences combining creator picks + latest signals + relevant news — frames buying decisions

### Cadence
- Daily news refresh
- Macro summary regenerates weekly or on demand
